# V2 Startup Incubation & Consulting Portal

Welcome to the official intake portal for **V2 Startup Incubation & Consulting**.

This interactive onboarding gateway is designed to guide founders, startups, and corporate clients through a structured discovery process to qualify venture concepts and align them with the optimal advisory track.

---

## 🎯 Corporate Pathway (Decide ➔ Build ➔ Raise)

Our consulting framework aligns venture development with structured milestone tracks:

* **DECIDE (Idea Validation - IGNITE):** Assess market viability, customer profile fit, and feasibility early in the venture lifecycle.
* **BUILD (Strategic Blueprint - BLUEPRINT BUILD):** Formulate comprehensive commercial business plans, pricing configurations, and initial go-to-market strategies.
* **RAISE (Investment Readiness - INVESTOR READY PRO):** Refine financial models, pitch deck materials, valuations, and capital raising roadmaps.

---

## 🔒 Confidentiality & Data Security

We prioritize data security and IP protection. The intake portal collects baseline operational parameters needed for lead qualification and path matching:
* Fully secured submission channels.
* Strict internal NDA guidelines govern all reviewed proposals.
* Sensitive project documents and proprietary files are handled later in our discovery process via secured data channels.
